Hi, I'm Sihan Long, a master's student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Brown University. I previously studied Statistics and Data Science at the University of Macau.

I'm interested in how AI agents learn to use tools, find the right information, and follow through on tasks that take more than a few steps. My recent projects explore reinforcement learning for tool use, long-term memory, and retrieval. I enjoy working through both the learning problem and the engineering needed to make an agent useful in practice.

During my internship at KPMG's AI Innovation Center, I worked on a coding agent that helps developers find and understand reusable code on GitHub, along with the backend for a policy research assistant. These experiences got me thinking more carefully about how agents handle incomplete information, recover when something goes wrong, and make their work easy to check.

You can find what I've been building on [GitHub](https://github.com/DonSteven). If you're working on similar ideas or just want to chat, feel free to [reach out](mailto:steven.long05@outlook.com)!
